# Editorial knowledge

## Purpose

Share practical knowledge about AI and software engineering. Keep a sober technical design. Use short sentences, clear terms, examples, and visuals that help readers understand and remember. Avoid sales language and engagement counters.

## Source treatment

Use private work as a research base, not as text to copy publicly. A default confidential tag does not establish confidentiality. Examine the actual content, ownership, reuse rights, identities, screenshots, business examples, and private links. Re-express useful knowledge with independent public references. Keep original publication, revision, test, and sharing dates separate. Unknown dates remain unknown.

Group duplicates and related formats under one subject before drafting. Keep the best supported text and its provenance. A newer filesystem timestamp does not prove a better source. Preserve historical measurements and record corrections separately. Cite each public source beside the claim it supports.

## Content types and dates

- News and magazine editions: three calendar months from first publication. Show Outdated news at expiry. Preserve the article in the archive. Do not reset the date after an edit.
- Guides: last edited and last technically reviewed are different fields. Review after six months by default. Use three months for fast-changing product instructions. Show Review due when late. Mark known problems immediately instead of waiting for the timer.
- Experiments: record test date, versions, method, questions, expected facts where relevant, results, cost, and limits. Do not generalize an old result to a newer product version.

## Visual and media rules

Every page needs a visual that explains a relationship, sequence, comparison, example, or timeline. Give it a useful caption and an accessible text equivalent. Keep text readable on mobile. Prefer visual HTML with optional narration when useful. Narration must be optional, with readable text and a transcript. Do not autoplay audio. Do not add nonworking play buttons.

Existing videos may be obsolete. Review all frames, narration, captions, product versions, and privacy before reuse. No old video is approved by its presence in the catalogue. The selected optional video host is YouTube; comments should be off. There is no promise about advertisement relevance.

## Technical accuracy

The LLM selects actions. The agent executes requested actions through the harness and returns results. KiroCrew is a gateway above harnesses. Do not describe the agent as thinking or deciding. Agent Commander is the author's working category, not an established industry standard.

Distinguish a vendor roadmap, documented capability, tested behavior, interpretation, and unverified claim. Compare enterprise cloud solutions by explicit requirements and dated evidence. Present home-built applications as proofs of concept. Do not imply they are maintained products.

## Publication sequence

1. Select a topic from the private catalogue and resolve duplicates.
2. Draft a new public-safe explanation with source dates and references.
3. Add the required explanatory visual and accessible caption.
4. Review accuracy, rights, privacy, format, and narration or video where present.
5. Obtain article-level publication approval. A source-selection yes is not approval to publish.
6. Add approved metadata and review dates to `content/catalogue.json`.
7. Run the build and targeted checks. Check desktop and mobile rendering.
8. Commit only task files with the approved public identity. Push the named release branch.
9. Read the deployment result and verify the public page. Update the private execution ledger with observed evidence.

## Maintenance

The generated maintenance ledger lists every published page, its content type, last review or test, next action, and due date. The catalogue is its source of truth. A scheduled rebuild updates age labels only. A technical review requires actual source and content checks. Record what was checked and any changes. Revisions must not silently erase old measurements.

The publishing Bot owns this process. It must not publish an unapproved item, export its private knowledge, install apps, or change KiroCrew core code to perform it.
